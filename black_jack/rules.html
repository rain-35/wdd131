How to Play Blackjack

Welcome to Blackjack 21. The goal is to beat the dealer by getting closer to 21 without going over. This page explains the rules, card values, and how each round works.

Goal of the Game

Try to finish with a higher total than the dealer without going over 21. If your hand goes above 21, you bust and lose the round.

Card Values

  • Number cards from 2 to 10 are worth their face value.
  • Jack, Queen, and King are each worth 10.
  • Aces count as 11 unless that would cause a bust, then they count as 1.

How a Round Works

  1. Click New Game to begin.
  2. You and the dealer each receive two cards.
  3. One of the dealer’s cards stays hidden at first.
  4. Click Hit to draw another card.
  5. Click Stand to keep your current hand.
  6. After you stand, the dealer takes their turn automatically.

Dealer Rules

The dealer follows fixed rules. The dealer draws cards until reaching at least 17, then stands. If the dealer goes over 21, the dealer busts and you win.

Winning the Round

  • If you bust, you lose.
  • If the dealer busts, you win.
  • If neither side busts, the higher total wins.
  • If both totals are equal, the round is a tie.

Quick Tips

  • Low totals are usually safer to hit on.
  • Standing on a strong total can help avoid busting.
  • Pay attention to the dealer’s visible card before deciding.
